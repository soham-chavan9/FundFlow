## Scope

The public landing page at `/`. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. It inherits the app's settled visual world (see the app-shell brief); this brief owns only the landing's purpose and composition.

## Audience and task

A recruiter or developer opening the portfolio link, and anyone who tracks personal spend. They should understand in one viewport what FundFlow does (track spending, budgets per category paced against the month, reports, an assistant grounded in their own data) and be one click from creating an account or signing in. No demo account: the page itself has to show the product.

## Constraints

No invented proof: no testimonials, user counts, ratings, press or savings figures. Sample data is labelled as sample. Works in en/es/zh, light and dark, at 375px, WCAG 2.2 AA. Signed-in visitors get "Open FundFlow" instead of sign-up.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern-banking product page played straight: say what it is, show the real product doing it, ask for the account. It refuses invented social proof and decorative chrome standing in for the product.

OWN-WORLD: The app's world: cool near-white plane, navy ink, one indigo accent for actions and the pace line, hairline borders, 8px radius, Geist with tabular figures; category hues only in data. Product fragments are the real components with sample data.

STORY: The visitor reads the one-line promise, sees a live-looking overview with budgets pacing against the month, scrolls through adding an expense, budget pace, insights and the assistant, and creates an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with wordmark, language and theme, Sign in, Create account. Headline and one sentence left-aligned, primary "Create a free account" and "Sign in" under it. Below, full content width, a framed product overview: summary strip, budget bars with the pace line, six-month trend, labelled sample.

FORM: canon, the category standard at full craft. seed: none (pinned). Quoted pin: "Standard fintech page (Recommended): Mercury-style and played straight: headline, sign-up, a real product screen, then a few feature sections with product fragments and a closing call to action." Craft bar: Mercury.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
